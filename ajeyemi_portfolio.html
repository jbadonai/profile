About Me

I'm Alajede Joseph Ajeyemi, a Senior BSS Engineer at 9Mobile (Etisalat Nigeria) in Port Harcourt, and a self-driven software developer with a passion for building tools that eliminate repetitive manual work. My professional journey started in civil and structural engineering, pivoted into telecommunications infrastructure, and has steadily expanded into full-stack software development.

Over the past 15+ years at 9Mobile, I've led end-to-end BSS site deployments, coordinated Huawei and Nokia vendors, delivered in-house LTE rollouts, and acted as Delegation of Authority for the Regional Rollout Manager. That engineering discipline — rigorous, detail-obsessed, deadline-driven — flows directly into how I write code.

On the development side, I've built desktop applications, web apps, REST APIs, WordPress plugins and themes, Python automation tools, and algorithmic trading systems coded in both Pine Script (TradingView) and MQL5 (MetaTrader 5). I thrive in the space between domain knowledge and software — the kind of problem that needs someone who's stood on a telecom site and watched a trade execute in real time.

My goal is to keep building practical, production-ready software that solves real problems — for businesses, traders, and individuals who need tools that actually work.
